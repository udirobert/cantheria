Cantheria — the problem01
the problem
Who checks the story
your agents tell you?
In the OpenAI × Hugging Face swarm incident, investigators delegated analysis to AI agents reading transcripts the swarm itself could write. The analysts were part of the record they audited — and nothing mechanically checked their conclusions.
02
Cantheria — the turn02
the instrument
The model proposes.
SQLite decides.
S — SEGMENT

Normalize

Swarm records land in one SQLite store — agent-days, threads, sessions — untrusted text fenced as data.

I — INFER

Propose

The model drafts atomic claims and SELECT-only SQL probes. It proposes; it never testifies.

F — FALSIFY

Decide

An evidence oracle checks grounding, attribution, replication, and control — mechanically.

T — TRACE

Retain

Every attempt — failed, repaired, flaky, unverifiable — stays in an append-only journal. Replay needs no model key.

Cantheria — trust & limits06
reproducibility is not a vibe
180/180
verdicts reproduce keylessly on the public audit slice — no model key, no network, sqlite only.
  • Rejected, flaky, and unverifiable candidates stay in the journal — the verifier audits itself.
  • confirmed means the committed probe passed its checks — not proof of every clause of the narrative.
  • Honest coverage: abstention is reported, never hidden — selective accuracy over brute force.
  • Runs on collusion.wiki, AI Village, and SwarmTraces — corpus-agnostic, offline-replayable.
